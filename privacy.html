Aegient Ltd Privacy Notice

This privacy notice explains how Aegient Ltd collects and uses personal data in connection with Quiet, Quiet Protect and the getquiet.co.uk website. It reflects the position as at 20 October 2026. It also explains the rights available to individuals under UK data-protection law.

2. The services covered by this notice

2.1 Quiet

Quiet is an email-triage service for freelancers, sole traders and small-business users. With the user’s authorisation, Quiet connects to the user’s mailbox and uses AI-assisted scoring to route incoming email into categories such as Act, Handled or Suppressed.

2.2 Quiet Protect

Quiet Protect is an email-safety service intended to help protect an elderly or otherwise vulnerable adult (the “protected person”) from scams, phishing and other potentially harmful email. It connects to the protected person’s mailbox with their agreement and uses AI-assisted scoring to assess incoming email before delivery. A trusted family member or carer (the “family manager”) operates a restricted oversight dashboard.

The family manager may see the sender’s identity, the routing outcome and a plain-language threat reason for messages that require attention. The family manager cannot see the email subject line, body or any attachment through Quiet Protect.

2.3 Our website

This notice also covers personal data processed when someone visits getquiet.co.uk or contacts us through the website or by email.

3. Whose personal data we process

Depending on the service and the circumstances, we may process personal data relating to:

  • Quiet users;

  • protected persons using Quiet Protect;

  • family managers and prospective co-managers using Quiet Protect;

  • people who send email to a Quiet or Quiet Protect mailbox; and

  • people who visit our website, contact us or make an enquiry.

4. Personal data we collect and where it comes from

4.1 Account and relationship information

We may collect names, email addresses, display names, account identifiers, service roles, subscription or account status, trusted-sender and blocked-sender entries, co-manager invitation status, and records of account or dashboard actions. This information is provided by the relevant Quiet user or Quiet Protect family manager, generated through use of the service, or obtained from the connected mailbox account.

4.2 Mailbox and message information

Where a mailbox is connected, we process information needed to provide the service. This may include the sender’s name and email address, recipient information, subject line, plain-text message body, HTML message body, date and time received, message identifiers, attachment filenames, types and sizes, sender history and information about how the message was routed or handled. Attachment content is processed when a protected person opens an attachment and is not stored by us. Quiet stores message information required to provide and improve its triage functions.

4.3 AI-generated and service-generated information

We generate and store information such as importance, urgency, scam or threat scores; confidence ratings; signal labels; routing outcomes; threat explanations; and records of Quiet user or Quiet Protect family manager overrides and other interventions.

4.4 Authentication and technical information

We process mailbox provider OAuth access and refresh tokens and related permission information so that the services can access the connected mailbox under the permissions granted during setup. We may also process IP address, request path, date and time, browser or device information, diagnostic information and security logs when a person uses our website or services.

4.5 Information obtained from someone other than the individual

We receive personal data indirectly in several circumstances. A family manager may initially provide a protected person’s name and email address. A manager may invite a co-manager. If a person sends email to a mailbox connected to Quiet or Quiet Protect, we receive that person’s name, email address and message information from the mailbox provider.

We use indirectly obtained information only for the purposes described in this notice. We may not be able to give this notice directly to every email sender. The notice is available at getquiet.co.uk, and anyone may contact us at privacy@getquiet.co.uk for further information or to exercise applicable rights.

4.6 Special-category and sensitive information

Email content may reveal sensitive information, including health, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, sex life or sexual orientation, or other highly personal matters. We do not ask our users to include such information and do not use it to infer those characteristics. It may nevertheless be processed incidentally where it appears in an email handled by the service. Users provide explicit consent to this incidental processing of sensitive information.

6. AI-assisted scoring and routing

Quiet and Quiet Protect use AI-assisted scoring to analyse message information and support routing. For Quiet, the service assesses matters such as importance and urgency and routes messages into the applicable triage category. For Quiet Protect, it assesses potential scam and threat signals and may route a message for delivery, review or blocking.

The scoring may take account of the sender, subject line, plain-text body, sender history and patterns or signals within the message. The service records relevant scores, confidence information, signal labels and routing outcomes.

A user or, where applicable, a family manager can review and override relevant routing outcomes. If you want information about a routing outcome or wish to challenge it, contact privacy@getquiet.co.uk.

7. Who receives personal data

We lawfully disclose personal data only where necessary to provide, support and secure the services, comply with law or protect legal rights. The principal recipients and service providers are:

  • Mailbox providers. We access connected mailboxes under delegated permissions granted during setup. Mailbox providers process data under their own terms and privacy information.

  • Amazon Web Services Bedrock: our AI-scoring provider. The information sent for scoring includes the sender’s identity, subject line and plain-text message body. Amazon Web Services states that neither it nor third-party model providers use customer inputs to, or outputs from, Amazon Bedrock to train Amazon Nova, Amazon Titan or any third-party models.

  • Railway: hosting provider for our backend service and production PostgreSQL database.

  • Amazon Web Services Simple Email Service: used in its London region for limited transactional messages, including co-manager invitation and account-coordination messages. Those messages may include the recipient’s email address, subject line and a short message body, but not protected-person email content or AI-scoring output.

  • Cloudflare: used for DNS, email routing and static website delivery. Cloudflare may process limited network and access-log information such as IP address, request path and time.

If you would like to learn more about the processing activities of these companies or their products and services, then you should refer to the privacy information on their websites.

We may also disclose information to professional advisers, regulators, courts, law-enforcement bodies or other authorities where necessary and lawful, and to a purchaser or prospective purchaser in connection with a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

For Quiet Protect, a family manager receives only the restricted information described in section 2.2. The family manager does not receive the email body through Quiet Protect.

9. How long we keep personal data

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, including providing the service, meeting legal and regulatory requirements, resolving disputes and establishing, exercising or defending legal claims. Retention depends on the type of information and the status of the account.

  • Active accounts: we retain account, message, routing and related service information while needed to provide the service and maintain its security and reliability.

  • Removed protected-person accounts: operational data is ordinarily kept in a recoverable, soft-deleted state for up to 30 days and is then permanently deleted through our scheduled deletion process. A formal erasure request may be handled separately and may bypass that restoration period where applicable.

  • Backups: deleted information may remain in protected point-in-time backups for a limited additional period until those backups expire or are overwritten. Backup copies are not used for ordinary business purposes.

  • Invitations: invitation links are intended to expire after seven days. We retain only limited invitation records for account administration, security and accountability for as long as necessary for those purposes.

  • Security and audit records: we retain restricted records of authority-changing and security-relevant events where necessary to protect the service, demonstrate accountability and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Access is restricted, and family managers cannot view the audit log.

When we permanently delete an account, we delete the OAuth token stored by Aegient. This does not itself revoke the permission recorded by the mailbox provider; the mailbox owner may also need to revoke access through their account settings.

Please contact us if you would like more information about how long we keep personal data.

10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. Measures include delegated OAuth authentication, role-based restrictions, application-enforced limits on family-manager access, encryption in transit, restricted administrative access, security logging and controlled deletion processes. No internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your data-protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • ask for access to your personal data and information about how it is used;

  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal data;

  • ask us to erase personal data;

  • ask us to restrict the use of personal data;

  • receive personal data you provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and ask us to transmit it to another controller, where the right to portability applies;

  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, including relevant profiling; and

  • ask for information about, and challenge, AI-assisted routing that concerns you.

These rights are not absolute. For example, we may retain limited information where necessary to comply with law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. If we cannot comply with a request, we will explain why.

Protected persons may withdraw their explicit consent to processing at any time.

To exercise a right, email privacy@getquiet.co.uk. We may need information to verify your identity and locate the relevant data. We normally respond within one month. Where permitted by law, that period may be extended by up to two further months for a complex request or multiple requests; if so, we will tell you within the first month.
