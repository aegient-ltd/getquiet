Quiet Protect

Email scam protection for an elderly parent, or anyone vulnerable to scams, with a family member or carer looking out for them.

Quiet Protect is running in a small family pilot and is not yet open to new families.

Free weekly scam alerts

Our weekly email describes a scam that’s going round, with simple words you can use to warn the person you look out for. It’s free, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

How it works

  1. You set it up. A family member or carer creates a manager account and adds the person they look out for.
  2. They agree to it themselves. They sign in to their own email account and read a plain-English summary of what Quiet will do. Nothing happens until they agree, and they can stop it at any time.
  3. Quiet checks every new email. Safe email arrives as normal. Quiet holds back anything it thinks is unsafe and sends you a short email alert.
  4. You decide. You can release a held email to their inbox, mark the sender as trusted, or block them.

What they see

They read their email in Quiet’s own simple inbox, in a web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. It shows the email Quiet thinks is safe and nothing it has held back. They can read, reply to, forward, and write emails as usual.

Quiet protects the emails they read in Quiet, so they should use Quiet’s inbox instead of their usual email app.

What you see

  • For each email that Quiet holds back: who sent it, when it arrived, and what Quiet decided and why, in Quiet’s own words.
  • The senders they trust, block, or often hear from, with how often each one writes and how often they reply.
  • How much mail and marketing they receive, naming the senders who keep sending pushy marketing emails, even when those emails reach their inbox.
You never see a subject line, a message or an attachment.

What you can do

  • Release a held email to their inbox, or release it and trust the sender.
  • Block a sender so their emails are held back from now on.
  • Trust a sender so their emails arrive without being checked, as long as they really come from that sender.
  • Ask Quiet to unsubscribe them from marketing they don’t want, where the sender allows it.

What it can’t do yet

  • No email check catches everything. When Quiet isn’t sure, it holds the email back instead of guessing, so you can decide.
  • Quiet doesn’t look inside attachments when it checks an email.
  • An email made entirely of pictures is hard to check, so one from a sender they rarely hear from is held back for you.
  • It works with Microsoft email accounts today. Gmail and Yahoo need Google’s and Yahoo’s approval first.

Where their email goes

An AI model running in Amazon Web Services’ London region checks each new email. Amazon states that neither it nor the model’s maker uses the checked content to train their models.

We keep emails for a set period, then delete them, and the person you protect can request their information at any time. The privacy notice has the full details.
